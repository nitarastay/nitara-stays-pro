forest of oak and rhododendron creates an atmosphere of profound stillness.

DESTINATION 2: CHAULI KI JALI
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Tagline: "A sacred rock lattice and cliff edge with the Himalayas at your feet"
Distance: 2 km from Nitara | Altitude: 2,200m | Best Season: Oct–Jun | Entry: Free
Description: Chauli ki Jali is a natural rock formation with a latticed appearance 
adjacent to the Mukteshwar Temple complex. The name translates roughly to "net of 
the she-bear" in Kumaoni. It is considered sacred and is believed to bless 
childless couples who pass through the narrow rock gap. Beyond its spiritual 
significance, the site offers one of the most dramatic cliff-edge viewpoints in 
all of Kumaon — a sheer drop into forested valleys with the full arc of the 
Himalayan range visible on clear days. Rock climbing and rappelling are available 
at this site for adventure seekers. The sunset views from this point are 
among the finest in all of Uttarakhand.
Highlights: Natural rock lattice formation | Sheer cliff viewpoint | 
Rock climbing & rappelling | Sacred site | Himalayan panorama

DESTINATION 3: JAGESHWAR DHAM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Tagline: "A valley of 124 ancient Shiva temples lost in Himalayan deodar forest"
Distance: 35 km from Nitara | Altitude: 1,870m | Best Season: Apr–Jun, Sep–Nov | Entry: Free
Description: Jageshwar Dham is one of the most significant Shiva pilgrimage sites 
in India — a complex of 124 ancient temples nestled in a dense deodar cedar forest 
in the Jataganga river valley. Dating back to the 7th–12th centuries AD, these 
temples represent the pinnacle of Nagara style temple architecture in the Himalayas. 
The main shrine houses a naturally formed Shiva lingam and is believed to be one 
of the twelve Jyotirlingas by some traditions. The forest surrounding the temples 
is protected and home to diverse birdlife. The drive to Jageshwar from Mukteshwar 
passes through stunning mountain scenery and apple orchards. A dawn visit when 
temple bells echo through the deodars is an unforgettable experience.
Highlights: 124 ancient temples | 9th–12th century Nagara architecture | 
Sacred deodar forest | Jyotirlinga shrine | Archaeological museum nearby

DESTINATION 4: KASAR DEVI
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Tagline: "A hilltop shrine where Van Allen belts meet Himalayan spirituality"
Distance: 45 km from Nitara | Altitude: 2,116m | Best Season: Oct–Mar | Entry: Free
Description: Kasar Devi is a small but profoundly powerful temple dedicated to 
Goddess Kasar Devi, perched on a spur of the Kashyap Hill above Almora. The site 
gained global fame in the 1960s and 70s when it became a gathering place for 
seekers, philosophers and artists including Swami Vivekananda, Bob Dylan, Cat Stevens, 
and Timothy Leary — earning the area the nickname "Crank's Ridge." Scientists have 
noted that the area lies within a Van Allen radiation belt zone similar to Stonehenge 
and Machu Picchu, which may contribute to its unique energy. The views of the 
snow-capped Himalayan range from here are extraordinarily vast — stretching from 
Kedarnath to Nepal on clear days. The small village below retains an atmosphere 
of quiet bohemian calm.
Highlights: Ancient Devi shrine | Van Allen belt energy zone | 
Himalayan panorama | Bohemian cultural history | Stunning sunrises

DESTINATION 5: BHALUGAAD WATERFALL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Tagline: "A hidden cascade tumbling through ancient oak forest into crystal pools"
Distance: 12 km from Nitara | Altitude: 1,800m | Best Season: Jul–Oct | Entry: ₹50
Description: Bhalugaad Waterfall is one of Mukteshwar's most beautiful natural 
attractions — a powerful cascade falling approximately 25 metres into a series of 
crystal-clear pools surrounded by dense oak and rhododendron forest. The trail to 
the waterfall winds through forests alive with birdcall and, in monsoon season, 
through meadows of wildflowers. The waterfall is most dramatic in the months 
following the monsoon when water volumes are at their peak and the surrounding 
forest is at its most lush and green. The pools at the base offer refreshing 
natural bathing spots. Wildlife including leopards has been sighted in the forests 
surrounding the waterfall, though sightings are rare. The trail is moderate and 
suitable for most fitness levels.
Highlights: 25-metre cascade | Natural rock pools | Forest wildlife | 
Wildflower meadows | Moderate trek trail

DESTINATION 6: KAINCHI DHAM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Tagline: "The ashram of Neem Karoli Baba — where Steve Jobs and Mark Zuckerberg sought wisdom"
Distance: 58 km from Nitara | Altitude: 1,400m | Best Season: Year-round | Entry: Free
Description: Kainchi Dham is the renowned ashram of the revered saint Neem Karoli 
Baba, set in a narrow gorge of the Kosi river valley near Bhowali. The ashram was 
established in 1964 and has since become one of the most visited spiritual sites in 
India — not just for Indian devotees but for seekers from across the world. Steve Jobs 
visited Kainchi Dham in 1974 before founding Apple. Mark Zuckerberg visited on the 
advice of Jobs himself. The ashram's energy is said to be exceptionally powerful, 
with many visitors reporting profound peace and clarity during their time here. 
The annual Bhandara festival in June draws tens of thousands of devotees. The 
surrounding valley with its rushing river and forested hillsides creates a 
naturally beautiful and calming setting.
Highlights: Neem Karoli Baba ashram | Visited by Steve Jobs & Zuckerberg | 
Kosi river valley | Annual June Bhandara festival | Profound spiritual energy
